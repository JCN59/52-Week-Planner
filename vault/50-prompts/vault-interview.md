# Prompt: Vault onboarding interview

**Use for:** setting up or refreshing a personal AI vault.

> I want to create a vendor-agnostic AI vault that acts as a shared brain across
> my tools, using markdown files and folders. Interview me one question at a time
> about: my daily life and roles, my work and active projects, repeated context,
> workflows I want AI to help with, and privacy boundaries. Then propose a vault
> structure (README.md, AGENTS.md, templates) and a migration plan. Ask me the
> first question now.

**Notes:** answer by voice and just talk — the agent structures it. This vault's
tailored version lives in `../INTERVIEW.md`.
