*"Interview me from INTERVIEW.md, one question at a time, then update the vault
from my answers."* Use a microphone and just talk — rambling is fine, the agent
will structure it. You can also answer inline below and ask an agent to process it.

**Agent instructions:** ask ONE question at a time, in order. Listen, ask a short
follow-up if something is vague, then move on. Afterwards: update
`10-personal/profile.md`, restructure `20-business/` and `30-projects/` to match
reality, seed `TASKS.md`, and log the setup in `DECISIONS.md`. Propose structure
changes before making them.
