# Selling It — turning ranked microsites into revenue from local companies

There are three distinct products you can sell once the machine works. Most people
run Model 1 first (it needs no sales skill — the ranked site does the selling),
then layer Models 2 and 3 on top.

---

## Model 1 — Pay-per-lead / rank-and-rent (you keep the asset)

You own the site, the domain, and the phone number. A local company pays for the
leads or rents the ranking.

**How it works:** the tracking number forwards to the partner company. Every call
is recorded and logged. You bill per qualified lead, or a flat monthly rent for
the whole site.

**Pricing anchors (adjust to ticket size):**
| Niche ticket size | Per qualified call | Flat monthly rent |
|---|---|---|
| $1,500–$3,000 (water heaters, leak detection) | $35–$75 | $500–$1,500 |
| $3,000–$10,000 (slab leak repair, sewer, roofing) | $75–$150 | $1,000–$3,000 |
| $10,000+ (foundation, remodel) | $150–$300 | $2,500–$6,000 |

Rule of thumb: charge 5–10% of average job value per booked-job-quality lead, or
price monthly rent at roughly 30–50% of what the same lead volume would cost the
company in Google Ads.

**Why companies say yes:** they already buy leads from HomeAdvisor/Angi at similar
prices — but those leads are sold to 3–5 competitors simultaneously. Yours are
**exclusive**, local, and provable (call recordings, durations). An 8-minute-50
call is a qualified lead and everyone knows it.

**The sales motion (proof-first):**
1. Build and rank FIRST. Never sell a promise; sell a ringing phone.
2. While ranking, answer the early calls yourself or route to one "beta" partner
   free for 2–4 weeks to build the call log.
3. Then call the 5–10 best local companies in the niche (good reviews, answers
   their phone, licensed): *"I own a website that ranks for [service] in [city].
   It generated N calls last month — here are three recordings. I'm looking for
   one company to take them exclusively."*
4. Scarcity is real, not a tactic: one site, one partner. If they pass, the next
   company on the list gets the calls.

**Contract essentials:** definition of a "qualified lead" (e.g. caller in service
area, >60 seconds, service-intent — with recording as arbiter); month-to-month;
you keep the domain/site/number; either side can walk; partner must be licensed
and insured (see compliance doc — in Texas plumbing this is not optional).

---

## Model 2 — Build-and-transfer (sell the site to the company)

You build the microsite FOR a specific company from day one, on their behalf, and
they own it.

**Pricing:** $3,000–$8,000 one-time build (you can genuinely deliver in days what
agencies quote 6–8 weeks for) **plus** $300–$1,000/mo for hosting, upkeep, content
adds, and rank monitoring. The recurring part is the business; the build fee just
qualifies the buyer.

**Pitch:** *"Your main website ranks in [home city]. It will never rank in
[satellite city] — that's how Google works. I build satellite sites that capture
those markets. Here's one I own that does it"* (show your Model 1 site and its
call log as the demo).

**Upsell path:** one company, five cities = five microsites = five build fees +
five retainers. This is the STAR method sold as a productized service.

---

## Model 3 — Sell the SYSTEM itself (training / licensing)

Once you've built 3+ sites and have documented results, the playbook + prompt
library + your brain (wiki templates) is a product for other agencies and
ambitious operators: course/community ($50–$150/mo), done-with-you cohorts
($1,500–$5,000), or white-label builds for other agencies. Don't lead with this —
it only sells on the back of real, screenshot-able results from Models 1 and 2.

---

## Finding the buyers (for Models 1 & 2)

- Google "[service] [city]" and take the Map Pack + ads: those companies already
  pay for this demand — they're pre-qualified buyers.
- Filter: 4.0+ stars, 20+ reviews, answers the phone in under 4 rings, license
  verifiable. A partner who doesn't answer calls burns your leads and your data.
- Cold call > cold email in blue-collar niches. Owner picks up the phone; the
  words "I have customers calling me for [service] in [city] and no one to send
  them to" get a meeting every time.
- Prospecting tools (or the Vibe Prospecting MCP already connected to this
  workspace) can pull the list of local companies + contacts per niche/city.

## The compounding math

Each site: ~$30 domain + ~$30/mo hosting + your build time (first site: a couple
of days; with the brain: hours). A single site producing 15 qualified calls/mo at
$75 = $1,125/mo against ~$35/mo of costs. Ten sites is a real business; the
constraint is niche/city selection discipline, not build capacity — Claude removed
that bottleneck.

**Sequence to run:** Site 1 in your own metro (learn + proof) → sell those leads
(Model 1 revenue + case study) → use the case study to sell 2–3 build-and-transfer
deals (Model 2 cash) → reinvest into more Model 1 sites (the equity) → only then
consider Model 3.
