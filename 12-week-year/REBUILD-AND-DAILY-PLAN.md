We have **7.5 weeks left** in Cycle 1 (Weeks 5–12). This is the operating
system that fixes every failure in `JUNE-POSTMORTEM.md` and makes the daily
work concrete. Read once, then just follow the daily rhythm.

---

## 5 principles of the rebuild (each fixes a June failure)

1. **Measure PROFIT, not revenue.** Star Gazer's lag measure is now
   *net profit*, not sales. A $0 ad-spend week that nets $200 beats a $700
   revenue week that loses money.
2. **Organic first, paid only when proven.** No paid Meta spend until a
   specific creative earns organic traction AND clears a ROAS floor (below).
3. **Consistency beats intensity.** A smaller amount done *every week* beats a
   big Week-1 push followed by silence. Never a $0 week.
4. **The plan lives where you'll see it.** Daily actions are in THIS repo
   (reliable), not dependent on a Drive hand-off that failed in June.
5. **Protect the anchor.** Star Gazer is the proven earner → it never gets
   skipped. TMES next. CN Capital lightest.

---

## Paid-ad rules (so we never repeat June's loss)

- **Default: $0 paid.** Grow organic first.
- Before ANY boost: run `virality_predictor` on the creative — only scale ones
  that score well.
- If you boost: **hard cap $10/day**, and kill it in 72h unless it clears
  **ROAS ≥ 2.0** (>$2 sales per $1 spend, after you confirm COGS).
- Track every dollar: ad spend is a line item in the weekly score.

## Per-business rebuild

### ⭐ Star Gazer Swag Shop — *the profit engine (protect & scale)*
**Who it is:** novelty/patriotic trucker & working-man humor tees. Winners:
**"I Drop Big Loads"** (81% of June sales) and patriotic **"250 Years"** trucker
tees. Audience buys on humor + identity, impulse, mostly from social.
**Cycle goal (reframed): $3,000 revenue AND positive net profit by Aug 23.**
**Strategy:**
- Lead every week with a proven winner or close variant — don't chase new
  aesthetics.
- Grow with **organic** reels/posts (the double-take format works); use paid
  only under the rules above.
- **Raise AOV** with bundles (buy-2) and a small upsell — cheaper than buying
  more traffic.
- **Weekly Definition of Done:** 1 virality-scored hero creative · 4 scheduled
  posts (IG+FB) · 1 active promo/CTA · reply to all comments/DMs.
- **Metrics that matter:** net profit, ROAS (if paid), conversion %, AOV.

### 🏠 TMES — The Mortgage Exchange Service — *biggest $ per unit (was neglected)*
**Who it is:** residential mortgage brokerage. Growth engine = **referral
partners (realtors)** + past-client refis. Content builds trust; *outreach*
closes loans.
**Cycle goal: 6 funded loans by Aug 23.** (Needs a live pipeline — see math.)
**Strategy:**
- **Partner outreach is the #1 lead measure**: 15 realtor/partner touches/week.
  Over 7.5 weeks that's ~110 touches → realistic loan flow.
- Reactivate past clients for refis (10 follow-ups/week).
- Content = credibility, not the driver: 2 FB posts + 1 blog + 1 reel/week on
  the predefined theme.
- **Compliance always:** NMLS #, Equal Housing, no rate guarantees.
- **Metrics that matter:** partner touches, applications taken, loans funded.

### 🏢 CN Capital Group — *long cycle, build the pipeline (run lighter)*
**Who it is:** commercial mortgage / CRE finance. Long sales cycles → measure
**pipeline**, not closings, this cycle.
**Cycle goal: 3 deals in active pipeline by Aug 23.**
**Strategy:**
- 10 qualified property-owner/broker contacts/week; review 2 deal packages.
- LinkedIn is the primary channel; content = authority (2 posts + 1 video + 1
  ad/week on the predefined theme).
- **Metrics that matter:** qualified contacts, deals into pipeline.

## THE DAILY RHYTHM (repeats every week — this is your calendar)

Time-blocked for a solo owner. ~2–3 focused hours/day. One business gets the
spotlight each day; Star Gazer gets touched daily because it's the earner.

### 🟦 MONDAY — "Command Day" (~3 hrs) — the most important day
- **9:00–9:30** — `/score-week`: score last week vs 85%, update dashboard,
  read your vision. (Non-negotiable. Block it.)
- **9:30–10:00** — `/plan-week`: approve this week's 3 predefined themes.
- **10:00–12:00** — `/run-week`: batch-create the WHOLE week's content for all
  3 businesses (SGS hero + posts, TMES posts+blog+reel, CNC posts+video+ad).
  Schedule posts now so the week runs on autopilot.
- ✅ End of Monday = the entire week's content is made and scheduled.

### 🟩 TUESDAY — "Star Gazer Day" (~2 hrs)
- Confirm SGS posts are scheduled/live (IG + FB).
- Activate/refresh the week's promo or drop.
- Engage: reply to every comment & DM (this drives the algorithm + sales).
- Check Shopify: orders, inventory, and yesterday+today sales.
- Paid ads: only per the rules; check ROAS if any are running.

### 🟨 WEDNESDAY — "TMES Day" (~2.5 hrs)
- Publish TMES post #1 + the blog.
- **15 realtor/partner touches** (calls, DMs, coffees) — the core lead measure.
- **10 borrower/past-client follow-ups.**
- Log every touch. This is where loans come from.

### 🟧 THURSDAY — "CN Capital Day" (~2 hrs)
- Publish CNC post + ad creative (LinkedIn + FB).
- **10 owner/broker contacts.**
- **Review 2 deal packages**; move anything qualified into the pipeline.
- Log contacts.

### 🟪 FRIDAY — "Publish & Push Day" (~1.5 hrs)
- Publish the week's videos/reels (TMES + CNC + SGS).
- Engage across all three accounts.
- **Numbers check:** SGS week-to-date profit, TMES touches done vs 15, CNC
  contacts done vs 10. Fix any gap today.
- Schedule weekend SGS posts.

### ⬜ SATURDAY — light (~30 min)
- 1 Star Gazer post live; reply to DMs. That's it.

### ⬛ SUNDAY — OFF (Breakout block)
- Rest. Optional 15-min glance at Monday's predefined themes.
