# Cycle 1 — Predefined Weekly Plans (single source of truth)

> Why this exists: in June the weekly plan was generic ("post content"), so
> every Monday started with a blank page — and after Week 1 it stalled. This
> file **predefines every week** so `/plan-week` and `/run-week` execute a
> concrete brief instead of inventing one. Themes are drawn from each
> business's content pillars and June's real sales data.

## June's lesson, baked into the plan
- Star Gazer's **"I Drop Big Loads" tee drove 81% of June sales, 96% from
  social.** Patriotic/trucker tees also sold. → SGS themes lean into the
  proven winners, not new experiments.
- Sales went to **$0 the moment posting stopped.** → The rule below.

## The one rule: never post a $0 week
Priority order when time is tight (protect revenue first):
1. **Star Gazer** (proven revenue engine) — never skip.
2. **TMES** (your license, biggest deals).
3. **CN Capital** (longest sales cycle — okay to run lighter).

**Minimum Viable Week** (if you can only do one thing): publish **Star Gazer's
hero creative + its promo**. That alone kept the till ringing in June.

## Weekly Definition of Done (the same specific checklist every week)

**⭐ Star Gazer** — *anchor, do first*
- [ ] 1 hero creative for the week's theme (image or video), virality-scored
- [ ] 4 social posts scheduled across the week (IG + FB)
- [ ] 1 active promo / CTA (code or drop)
- [ ] Reply to comments & DMs within 24h

**🏠 TMES**
- [ ] 2 Facebook posts (1 on theme, 1 educational + CTA)
- [ ] 1 blog post on the theme
- [ ] 1 short reel/video
- [ ] 15 realtor/partner touches + 10 borrower follow-ups (logged)

**🏢 CN Capital**
- [ ] 2 posts (LinkedIn + FB) on the theme
- [ ] 1 video + 1 ad creative
- [ ] 10 owner/broker contacts + 2 deal packages reviewed

## How the weekly rhythm uses this file
- **Monday `/plan-week`** → reads this file, loads the week's 3 themes
  automatically (you just approve or swap), writes the week's plan file.
- **`/run-week`** → executes the Definition of Done against those themes.
- **Monday `/score-week`** → scores execution vs. 85%, updates the scoreboard.

No more rogue, invented weeks. Every Monday the brief is already written.
